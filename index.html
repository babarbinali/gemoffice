Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free? What's the catch?

There is no catch. No fee, no subscription, no adverts and no paid version. It was made for people who cannot afford to pay for software.

Do I have to give my email?

No. Your email is completely optional, when downloading and in the program. Click "Download without email" or "Skip" and start working.

How do I send a comment or an idea?

Every app has a 💬 Feedback button at the top. Write your comment and click Send. If there is no internet, it is sent automatically once you are connected.

Can I edit the words inside a PDF?

Yes. Choose the Edit Text tool, click on the words, then delete and type. The same font, size and colour are kept. For scanned PDFs, use Recognize Text first.

Will my files open in Microsoft Office?

Yes. It saves standard .docx, .xlsx and .pptx files, so anyone can open them in Microsoft Office, WPS or LibreOffice.

Can I share it with friends or install it at my school?

Yes, please do! Install it on as many computers as you like, and send this website's link to anyone who needs it.

Do I need the internet?

Only to download it. After installing, everything works offline.

Does it work on Mac or phones?

For now it runs on Windows 10 and 11 only.

What doesn't it support yet?

Old .doc, .xls and .ppt files, and pivot tables and macros in Excel files. Keep a copy of important files before saving over them.

Why does Windows or my antivirus warn me?

Because the program is new and not signed with a paid certificate. Only download it from this website.

Privacy

  • Your documents: nothing. Your files never leave your computer.

We use your email only to tell you about new versions, if you asked for that. We never sell or share your details. To have your details deleted, send us a comment from the program and ask.
